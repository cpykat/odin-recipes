Carrot Salad

Description

Our favorite. Quick and easy to make. Healthy, Delicious and perfect with fried POTATO.

Ingredients

  • Carrot
  • Honey
  • Soy Sauce
  • Rice Vinegar
  • Red Pepper
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Sesame Seeds
  • Spring Onion
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Peel the carrot in thin slices using vegetable peeler
  2. Mix honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, red pepper, ginger, smashed garlic, spring onion and sesame seeds
  3. Heat up the extra virgin olive oil, slowly pour it on the mixed ingredients and stir
  4. Put the finished sauce on the carrot, mix well, add some fresh spring onions and let it sit for five minutes
